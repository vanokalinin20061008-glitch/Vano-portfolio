03 How it's made

The model never decides. I do.

Most AI video is a slot machine — pull, pray, post. I work in a hybrid pipeline: the film is decided on paper and in 3D first, then every generated shot executes a decision that's already made, and the seams are finished by hand. What comes out is authored cinema, at series pace.

01

Story & series structure

Scripts, season arcs, hooks and cliffhangers — Heir is built as a 61-episode grid before a frame exists.

02

Previs in Unreal Engine 5

Scenes blocked in 3D: floor plans, the camera line, eyelines and lenses decided up front.

03

Locked cast & worlds

Characters, locations and light designed once and held across every shot and episode — no drift between cuts.

04

Directed generation

Each shot generated against a chosen camera move, performance beat and continuity of motion. The model executes; it doesn't choose.

05

Compositing & color

After Effects and DaVinci Resolve — seams removed, light matched, one grade across the series.

06

Sound, music & edit

Dialogue, sound design and score built per scene; cut to performance, not to a metronome.
